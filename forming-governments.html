Forming a government

Voters in a parliamentary democracy elect a legislature, not a government. When no party wins a majority, party leaders bargain over who governs. This tool works through the chapter’s Estonian example of how to predict the result, and then gives you elections of your own to work through, including some where the government that formed is not the one either prediction expects.
