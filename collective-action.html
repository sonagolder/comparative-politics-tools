Collective action theory

Why would anyone join a pro-democracy protest when they could stay home and still enjoy democracy if the protest succeeds? This tool works through Table 8.1 one scenario at a time, then lets you change the group and the stakes and practice reading the table yourself.

Moving B or C changes the payoffs, but as long as B is greater than C the underlines never move: in each scenario the same choice is your best response. The chapter adds that it seems reasonable to think people become less willing to participate as the cost rises, and more willing as the benefit grows.

Problem

2. What is your best response?

Each problem gives you your expectation about how many of the others will participate. As the chapter puts it, your decision depends on that conjecture.
