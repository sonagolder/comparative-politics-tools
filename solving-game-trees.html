Solving extensive form games

Extensive form games are solved by backward induction. Start at the end of the game tree: at each final choice node, work out which action gives the player making the choice the highest payoff, and make that branch bold. Then move backward to the choice node prior to it, where the player can look down the game tree and follow the bold lines to see what each of her actions leads to. Keep going until you reach the initial node, then read off the expected outcome, the payoffs, and the subgame perfect equilibrium.
