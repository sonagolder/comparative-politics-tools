Which cleavage gets politicized?

Every society is divided in many ways, but only some divisions become the lines along which parties compete. This tool follows the chapter’s argument that which division gets politicized depends on two things together: how people’s attributes are distributed, and how much of the vote it takes to win office. The walkthrough uses the chapter’s own hypothetical country, and Practice gives you ten countries to work through.
